Alle acht Themen besitzen ein Landschaftsbild und eine echte transparente Vordergrundebene. Die vorherigen Bilder bleiben unter `assets/scenery/` erhalten; eingebunden wird `assets/scenery/anime/`. Originalausgaben des Bildwerkzeugs wurden kopiert, nicht verschoben.

## Gemeinsamer Bearbeitungsprompt

Restyle this existing production game scenery asset into a polished hand-painted Pokemon anime background style. Preserve the exact camera, landscape layout, perspective, silhouettes, major terrain edges, flat standing surfaces and open central space, so existing separately rendered Pokemon and interactive buttons still align with their ground anchors. Visually transform the material treatment: simplified clean shapes, beautifully painted broad color masses, soft cel-shaded light and shadow, restrained smooth gradients, subtle ink-like edge definition. NOT photorealistic, no photographic texture, no gritty microdetail, no 3D render. Lively and welcoming high-quality Japanese creature-adventure anime art, detailed enough for a full-screen launcher without overpowering official Pokemon character artwork. Keep existing scene palette and season. No Pokemon, creatures, people, logos, letters, interface, watermark or new objects. Widescreen 16:9.

## Zusatz je Ebene

Landschaft: This is the opaque landscape background. Keep the center quiet and low detail for real interactive UI. Preserve floor areas so characters stand naturally on them.

Vordergrund: This is a TRANSPARENT FOREGROUND OVERLAY: preserve real alpha and fully empty transparent center, preserve separation of corner branches/foliage/rocks, do not add a background.
