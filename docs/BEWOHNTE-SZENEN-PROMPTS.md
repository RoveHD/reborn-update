## Gemeinsames Briefing

Create a finished production game BACKGROUND illustration by editing the first image. This is an inhabited Pokemon world in authentic clean 2D POKEMON ANIME style: crisp organic outlines, simple broad painted color shapes, restrained cel shading, charming accurate recognizable Pokemon designs. No photorealistic skin/fur/scales, no 3D render, no realism, no pasted sprite outlines. Integrate the following Pokemon directly into this landscape with matching lighting, atmospheric depth, accurate anatomy, contact shadows and natural overlap behind landscape elements. Preserve the first image's existing landscape, camera, season and quiet central clearing. SECOND reference if supplied is ONLY an example of natural composition/occlusion, NOT style or UI to copy. DO NOT paint ANY logo, text, buttons, panels, labels, interface, watermark or reference collage. The output must be ONLY ONE seamless 16:9 landscape illustration. Keep the central column from x=34% to66% entirely free of Pokemon and high-contrast clutter for live interface overlay; keep top10% clear. Place characters in left/right lower thirds, well inset from edges, no cropped heads or feet. At least one Pokemon partially concealed by rocks, flowers or snow with its face still visible. Different poses and directions, not all front facing. Official heights below are world-scale references: use distance and pose to adapt their screen size. Three characters at most, balanced and not crowded.

## Technische Ebenen

Die Illustration mit festen Pokémon bildet die Landschaftsebene. Transparente Zweige und der unterste Vordergrund bewegen sich stärker; Atmosphäre, Wetter und interaktive Requisiten besitzen eigene Ebenen. UI-Elemente bleiben unbewegt. Es handelt sich um 2,5D-Parallaxe, nicht um ein vollständiges 3D-Modell oder unabhängig animierte gemalte Pokémon.
